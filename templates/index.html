3 Golden Rules Every Senior Must Remember

1
UPI PIN is ONLY for giving money
Entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS subtracts money from your account. You NEVER enter a PIN to receive money.
2
Banks NEVER ask for OTP or Passwords
No bank manager, police officer, or telecom executive has the authority to ask for your SMS OTP or card details.
3
NEVER install AnyDesk / QuickSupport
If any caller asks you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or share your screen, disconnect immediately.
