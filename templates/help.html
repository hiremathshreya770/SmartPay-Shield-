Please Stay Calm — Do Not Blame Yourself

If you shared an OTP, clicked an unfamiliar link, or sent money by mistake, please take a deep breath. Sophisticated fraudsters use deceptive psychological pressure and fear to trap honest people of all ages. You are not at fault. What matters now is taking calm, decisive action right away.

What to Do If You Suspect Fraud: 7 Immediate Steps

Prioritized checklist to stop fraudsters and protect your bank account:

1

STOP All Contact Immediately

Hang up the phone immediately. Close WhatsApp calls, Telegram chats, or video calls. Do not argue, do not apologize, and do not listen to threats of "arrest" or "penalties". Disconnecting cuts off their psychological control.

2

NEVER Share Additional OTPs, UPI PINs, or Passwords

Even if a caller claims they are trying to "cancel the fraud" or "reverse the transaction", NEVER share any subsequent OTP or enter your UPI PIN. Fraudsters often attempt a second larger debit pretending it is a refund.

3

Contact Your Bank Immediately Through Verified Official Details

Call your bank's toll-free customer care number printed directly on the back of your physical ATM debit card or passbook. Request an urgent freeze on your bank account and debit card to prevent any further withdrawals.

4

Call Helpline 1930 Promptly ("Golden Hour")

If money was debited from your account, dialing 1930 immediately allows the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) to alert bank nodal officers and freeze the stolen funds in the fraudster's wallet or recipient account before they can withdraw it.

5

File an Official Complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

Submit an online complaint at the official portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in. Include your bank name, debit amount, transaction reference ID, and date. Note down your formal Acknowledgment Number for your police report.

6

Preserve All Evidence Safely

Do not delete suspicious messages or call logs. Take screenshots of fraudulent SMS messages, WhatsApp chats, payment receipts, and caller phone numbers. This evidence is vital for the bank and police investigations.

7

How to Find Genuine Bank Customer Care Numbers

Fraudsters frequently create fake Google search ads or fake Google Maps entries with their own phone numbers pretending to be "State Bank Customer Care" or "HDFC Helpline". Always verify numbers through independent physical channels:

✔ SAFE SOURCES
  • Back of your physical ATM Debit Card: Legitimate toll-free 1800 numbers are permanently printed on the card back.
  • Physical Bank Passbook or Chequebook: The branch phone number is printed on the first page.
  • Visiting your local branch in person and speaking with the bank manager.
✖ UNSAFE SOURCES
  • Random Google search results, Google Maps listings, or Justdial phone numbers.
  • Phone numbers sent to you in unsolicited SMS messages or WhatsApp forwards.
  • Numbers given by someone who called you first claiming to be customer service.
