Understanding QR Codes: The Truth About Receiving Money

Many senior citizens are tricked into scanning QR codes by fraudsters claiming it will credit a pension, refund, or prize. Here is the golden truth:

✔ GENUINE USAGE
  • You scan a QR at a shop to PAY for items you bought.
  • You see the registered merchant name on your screen.
  • Money leaves your account with your knowledge.
✖ FRAUDULENT TRAP
  • Someone on WhatsApp sends a QR saying: "Scan this to receive ₹5,000 refund/pension".
  • They claim entering your PIN will credit money.
  • TRUTH: Scanning and entering your PIN will DEDUCT money instantly!
⭐ The Unbreakable Rule of QR Codes:

You NEVER need to scan a QR code to receive money. If someone tells you to scan a QR code to get money, they are trying to steal from you.
